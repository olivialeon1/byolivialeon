My story

I'm going into my senior year at Cal Poly SLO, where I continue to study and grow as a graphic designer.

My interest in art began during my senior year of high school, when my teacher encouraged me to explore creativity. After graduating, I attended Cuesta College for two years and continued taking hands-on art classes.

During my final year at Cuesta, I took my first graphic design course and discovered a new way to combine art and technology. I fell in love with designing on the computer, transferred to Cal Poly, and have continued developing my skills ever since.

Outside of design

When I'm not designing, you can usually find me loving on my dogs, tanning at the beach, stopping by the nearest In-N-Out, or working on another arts and crafts project. Diamond painting is one of my favorites recently.
